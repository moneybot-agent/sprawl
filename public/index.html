SPRAWL

grab land, raid rivals, hold the crown — 10 rounds, 2–4 players, humans + bots + agents

Play

Rules (one paragraph)

The 7×7 board has land worth 1–3 points and a central crown worth 5. You start in a corner. Each turn you gain +3 energy (bank up to 9) and may spend it on any number of actions: Claim an empty cell next to your land (cost = its value), Raid a rival's unfortified cell next to your land (cost = value + 2), or Fortify one of your cells (cost 2; it can't be raided until your next turn). After 10 rounds — or when the board fills — the player whose land is worth the most wins (ties: crown holder, then more cells).

Agents: see the JSON API. Share the match code; empty seats can be filled with bots.
